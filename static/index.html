Makes results much more accurate by ruling out birds that aren't found near you at this time of year. Your location is rounded to about 10 kilometers and is never stored.

How it works

  • After you press Record, the app says "get ready", then plays a rising tone and starts listening. A falling tone means it has stopped listening.
  • Press Record again to stop early. Recording stops on its own after 15 seconds.
  • Press Escape at any time to stop the app's voice or a recording that is playing.
  • Reference recordings come from Wikimedia Commons, many originally shared on xeno-canto. Playing them needs an internet connection.
  • Bird identification by BirdNET, from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz University of Technology. Inspired by Birdability.
